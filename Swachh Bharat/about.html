Our Mission:
Clean India, Bright Future

Swachh Bharat is a student-led movement committed to environmental cleanliness, community engagement, and sustainable practices across India. We believe that lasting change starts with small, consistent actions.

Our Core Principles

Educate

Raising awareness about environmental issues through workshops, campaigns, and community programs in schools and neighborhoods.

Clean

Organizing regular cleanup drives in public spaces, streets, rivers, and parks to remove waste and restore natural beauty.

Sustain

Promoting long-term sustainable practices like composting, recycling, and reducing single-use plastics in daily life.

Our Journey

From a small group of students to a nationwide movement — here's how we've grown.

2019

The Beginning

Started with 15 students in a single school organizing weekend cleanup drives in local neighborhoods. Collected over 200 kg of waste in our first month.

School Program 15 Students
2020

Expansion to Multiple Schools

Partnered with 12 schools across three cities. Launched educational workshops on waste segregation and composting. Our volunteer base grew to 300+ students.

12 Schools 300+ Volunteers
2021

River Revival Project

Initiated monthly riverbank cleanup campaigns with water quality monitoring. Removed over 3 tons of plastic waste from local water bodies.

Environmental Impact 3 Tons Removed
2022-2023

Community & Campus Initiatives

Launched Plastic-Free Campus program across 45 institutions. Established community composting centers in 8 neighborhoods. Volunteer count reached 1,200+.

45 Campuses 8 Composting Centers
2024-Present

Nationwide Movement

Now active in 15 states with 3,800+ volunteers. Conducted 127 major campaigns, collected over 5,400 kg of waste, and impacted thousands of lives through education and action.

15 States 3,800+ Volunteers 127 Campaigns
