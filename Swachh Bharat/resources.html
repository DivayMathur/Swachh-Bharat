Quick Checklists

Fast reference guides for common tasks. Bookmark this page for quick access!

✅ Pre-Drive Essentials

  • Get location permissions (if public property)
  • Order gloves, bags, and safety gear
  • Promote on social media (2 weeks before)
  • Coordinate with waste disposal trucks
  • Brief volunteers on safety protocols

🎯 During the Drive

  • Set up registration desk with sign-in sheet
  • Take before photos from multiple angles
  • Segregate waste (plastic, paper, organic)
  • Weigh waste collected (track impact)
  • Take after photos and group pictures
